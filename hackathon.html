Event Overview

Codyssey is IISER TVM’s flagship hackathon, part of the science fest Anvesha. Join students, researchers, and tech enthusiasts to solve real-world problems using AI, ML, and more. From ideation to impact, build solutions that are innovative and practical.

  • Deadline: Nov 1, 2025 @ 12:30pm GMT+5:30
  • Mode: Online
  • Eligibility: Ages 16+, teams of 1–5, select countries, no companies/professionals
  • Beginner Friendly: Yes
  • Theme: Machine Learning/AI for Social Good
  • Host: IISER Thiruvananthapuram
  • Prize: Cash prize for best solution, certificates, mentorship, and more

Hackathon Structure

  1. Registration & Submission (Oct 5 – Nov 1, 2025): Teams (1–5) register and submit projects directly.
  2. Submission must include:
    • Problem Statement & Importance
    • Proposed Solution
    • Target Audience
    • Technical Approach & Tech Stack
    • Implementation (code + docs)
    • Demo Video/Presentation
    • Impact & Feasibility
    • Challenges & Mitigation

Judging Criteria

  • Novelty & Creativity: Uniqueness and innovation
  • Technical Soundness: Code quality, feasibility, implementation
  • Impact: Practical value and benefits
  • Presentation: Clarity and demonstration

Extra Notes

  • Interdisciplinary solutions encouraged
  • Think beyond code: combine research, creativity, and impact
  • Follow ethical coding and cite third-party resources
