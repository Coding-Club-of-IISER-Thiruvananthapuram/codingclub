CCIT · INNOVATING WITH CODE

Welcome to the Coding Club

We are a community of curious minds who research, explore, and build in ML, AI, data science, computational science, and coding. From learning new skills to turning ideas into projects, we experiment, collaborate, and solve problems together. Whether you are just taking your first steps into coding or already building sophisticated systems, join us as we learn, experiment, collaborate, and turn ideas into reality.
